Meet your coach

Training that feels personal, because it is.

I earned my degree in Exercise and Sport Science at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I was also on the Division I cheerleading team. That's where I built my foundation in strength training, conditioning, injury prevention and performance.

Over the years I've noticed that many people feel uncomfortable in big gyms, don't know where to start, or feel like programs aren't built for them. So I created a training experience that is fully personalized, private, and built around your goals, your schedule and your starting point.

My goal is simple: make fitness accessible, less intimidating, and something you actually look forward to.

"We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit."
