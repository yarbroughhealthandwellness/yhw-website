Refund policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Monthly plans

  • Plans are paid monthly for 4, 8 or 12 sessions.
  • Unused sessions roll over to the next month only, then expire.
  • Payments are not refunded in cash. Unused sessions carry forward instead.
  • You can stop your plan anytime before the next month starts.

Late cancellations

Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, and no-shows, count as used and aren't refunded or rolled over.

If I cancel

If I have to cancel a session, it's always rescheduled at no cost to you.
