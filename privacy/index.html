Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This page explains what information Yarbrough Health and Wellness collects through this website and how it's used.

What I collect

  • Information you send me. When you text, email or message me, I receive your name, phone number or email, and whatever you choose to share, such as your goals or health details.
  • Basic site data. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure.

How I use it

Only to reply to you, schedule and run your sessions, and keep your training plan. I don't sell or rent your information.

Third-party services

This site shows an Instagram feed and links to Instagram. When you view or click that content, Instagram (Meta) may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time.
