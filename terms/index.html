Policies

Terms of service

Last updated November 15, 2026

These terms cover personal training sessions with Yarbrough Health and Wellness (Lucie Yarbrough). Booking a session means you agree to them.

Sessions and plans

  • Sessions are 60 minutes, 1-on-1, in a private gym in Winston-Salem, in your home (Winston-Salem and nearby), or online.
  • Monthly plans include 4, 8 or 12 sessions and are billed monthly.
  • Your first session is free, with no obligation to buy a plan.

Cancelling and rescheduling

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your session by text or email.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and no-shows, count as a used session.
  • If I need to cancel, your session is rescheduled and never counted against you.

Unused sessions

Unused sessions roll over to the following month only. After that they expire. See the refund policy.

Health and safety

  • Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition, injury, or are pregnant or postpartum.
  • Tell me about any health changes, pain or injuries before each session.
  • Exercise carries some risk of injury. You take part voluntarily and agree to follow instructions and stop if something feels wrong.

In-home sessions

Please provide a safe, clear space to train. I bring the equipment needed for your session.

Online sessions

You're responsible for a safe training space and a stable internet connection.
